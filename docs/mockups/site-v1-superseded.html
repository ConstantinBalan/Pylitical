House Bill 5501 Introduced

A bill to amend the Natural Resources and Environmental Protection Act

This bill would require the Department of Environment, Great Lakes, and Energy to establish a grant program for replacing lead service lines. The department would award these grants to municipalities with populations under 50,000. It specifies that $4,200,000 from the water infrastructure fund would be allocated for this program in fiscal year 2027. Municipalities receiving funds would need to complete replacements within five years, and the department would submit annual reports to the legislature.

Sponsored by Rep. A. Nguyen · Read the bill text
House Bill 5502 Introduced

A bill to amend the Michigan Vehicle Code

This bill proposes changes to Michigan's vehicle code related to license plate renewal fees. It increases the standard fee for renewing a license plate from $10 to $15. This adjustment would apply to all vehicle owners required to renew their plates in the state, effective October 2026.
