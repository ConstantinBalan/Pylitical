House Bill 5501

A bill to amend the Natural Resources and Environmental Protection Act

This bill would require the Department of Environment, Great Lakes, and Energy to establish a grant program for replacing lead service lines. Grants would go to municipalities with populations under 50,000, funded by $4,200,000 from the water infrastructure fund in fiscal year 2027. Municipalities would have five years to finish replacements, and the department would report annually to the legislature.

Sponsored by Rep. A. Nguyen. Read the bill

House Bill 5502

A bill to amend the Michigan Vehicle Code

This bill raises the standard license plate renewal fee from $10 to $15, effective October 2026. It applies to all vehicle owners required to renew their plates in the state.

Sponsored by Rep. D. Okonkwo. Read the bill

Senate Bill 402

A bill to amend the Revised School Code

This bill changes how districts calculate instructional hours for students in career and technical education programs, allowing work-based learning to count toward the annual requirement. It takes effect at the start of the 2027 school year.

Sponsored by Sen. R. Alvarez. Passed the Senate 24 to 14. Read the bill
